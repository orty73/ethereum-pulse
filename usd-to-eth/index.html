USD to ETH conversion explained

This page helps you estimate how much Ethereum (ETH) you can buy with a given amount of US dollars (USD). Because USD to ETH depends on the live market price of Ethereum, the result is an estimate rather than a fixed quote. The calculator displays both the value in ETH and the equivalent in wei, the smallest unit used on the Ethereum blockchain.

Many users search for USD to ETH, dollars to Ethereum, how much ETH can I buy with dollars, or USD to Ethereum calculator. This page is built to answer those searches with a simple live tool and a clear explanation of the conversion process.

Formula

ETH = USD ÷ (ETH price in USD)
Wei = ETH × 1e18

For example, if Ethereum is trading at 3,000 USD and you enter 100 USD, the result is a fraction of one ETH. That value can also be converted into wei for full precision, which is useful in wallets, smart contracts, gas calculations, and blockchain applications.

Why the USD to ETH result changes

Ethereum prices move continuously on cryptocurrency markets. As a result, the amount of ETH you receive for a given dollar amount can increase or decrease throughout the day. If the market price of ETH rises, the same USD amount buys less Ethereum. If the price falls, the same dollar amount buys more ETH.

That is why this converter should be used as a market-based estimate. The refresh option helps you get the latest available ETH price before making a comparison or planning a transaction.

What is wei in Ethereum?

Wei is the smallest indivisible unit of Ethereum. It exists so that ETH values can be represented with exact precision instead of rounded decimals. This matters in crypto transactions, DeFi protocols, smart contracts, and any use case where exact amounts are required.

1 ETH = 1,000,000,000,000,000,000 wei. By showing both ETH and wei, this converter is useful not only for beginners but also for developers and advanced users who need precise blockchain denominations.
