How the conversion works

The conversion uses the current market price of Ethereum in euros and applies a simple formula.

ETH = 1000 ÷ (ETH price in EUR)

If Ethereum becomes more expensive in EUR, then 1000 euros buys less ETH. If the price falls, then 1000 euros buys more ETH. Because crypto markets move continuously, the result on this page can change over time.

What is wei?

Wei is the smallest unit of Ethereum and is used for exact precision in wallets, smart contracts, gas fees, token transfers, and blockchain calculations. 1 ETH = 1,000,000,000,000,000,000 wei.
