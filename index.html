What is Wei?

Wei is the smallest denomination of Ethereum. Using wei avoids floating-point rounding issues and allows exact representation of ETH values.

Ethereum Pulse provides fast, simple and accurate tools to convert ETH or fiat currencies into wei using deterministic formulas or live market prices.
