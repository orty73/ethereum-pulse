What is wei?

Wei is the smallest denomination of Ethereum. Instead of dealing with tiny decimals like 0.00001234 ETH, wei makes exact Ethereum amounts easier to represent.

This website provides simple tools to convert ETH or fiat currencies into wei, using either fixed formulas or current market prices.

Security, decentralization and scalability

Ethereum’s roadmap is often described with the “blockchain trilemma”: security, decentralization, and scalability. The modern approach relies on rollups (L2) plus better data availability on L1 (blobs / PeerDAS), with zk proofs increasingly used for verification.
