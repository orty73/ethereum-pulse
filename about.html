About Ethereum Pulse

Ethereum Pulse is a minimalist website created to make Ethereum easier to understand. The goal is to provide essential Ethereum data in a clean, readable, and accessible format.

Many crypto websites are overloaded with charts and technical information. Ethereum Pulse focuses only on the most important indicators so anyone can quickly understand the state of the Ethereum network.

Ethereum Pulse is an independent project and is not affiliated with the Ethereum Foundation.
