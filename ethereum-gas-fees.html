Ethereum Gas Fees

A simple explanation of gas fees: what they are, why they change, and how to reduce costs. For live network stats, open Ethereum Pulse.

What is gas on Ethereum?

Gas is a unit that measures the computation needed to process actions on Ethereum. Simple transfers use little gas, while smart contract interactions use more.

Why do gas fees rise?

Gas fees rise when many users compete for limited block space. If demand is high, you need to pay more to be included faster. When the network is quieter, fees often fall.

Base fee vs priority fee (simple)

  • Base fee: adjusts with network demand and is required.
  • Priority fee (tip): extra incentive to get included faster.
  • Total cost: depends on fee level and gas used by your transaction.

How to reduce gas fees

  • Try off-peak times when the network is less busy.
  • Avoid urgency if you can wait for lower fees.
  • Use Layer 2 networks when available for cheaper execution.
  • Batch actions when your wallet or app supports it.

FAQ

What are Ethereum gas fees?

Gas fees are transaction costs paid to use Ethereum (transfers, swaps, smart contracts).

Why do gas fees change?

Fees rise when demand for block space is high and fall when network activity decreases.

How can I reduce gas fees?

Use off-peak times, avoid urgency, and use Layer 2 networks for cheaper transactions.
