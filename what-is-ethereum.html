What Is Ethereum?

Ethereum is a decentralized blockchain platform that allows developers to build applications without relying on centralized authorities.

What Is ETH?

ETH is the native cryptocurrency of the Ethereum network. It is used to pay transaction fees and interact with decentralized applications.

How Does Ethereum Work?

Ethereum uses a distributed network of validators to process transactions and secure the blockchain through a consensus mechanism.

Why Is Ethereum Important?

Ethereum enables decentralized finance, digital ownership, and programmable money used across many industries worldwide.
