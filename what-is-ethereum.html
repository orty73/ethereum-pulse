What Is Ethereum?

A beginner-friendly guide to Ethereum: what it is, what ETH does, and why people use the network. For live stats, visit Ethereum Pulse.

Ethereum, in simple terms

Ethereum is a blockchain network. Like Bitcoin, it can transfer value. But Ethereum can also run smart contracts: programs that execute on-chain and enable decentralized applications.

What is ETH?

ETH is the native asset of Ethereum. It is used to pay for transactions (gas) and helps secure the network. Many Ethereum apps also use ETH for liquidity and settlement.

What are smart contracts?

Smart contracts are pieces of code stored and executed on the blockchain. They can hold assets, enforce rules, and interact with other contracts—without needing a centralized server.

  • DeFi: lending, trading, stablecoins
  • NFTs: digital ownership and collectibles
  • DAOs: on-chain governance and coordination

Why do people pay gas fees?

Gas fees pay for computation and help the network prioritize transactions. When demand is high, fees increase. When demand falls, fees become cheaper.
