Frequently Asked Questions

Is EUR to ETH conversion exact?

No. EUR to ETH uses a market price, so results are estimates and may change as the ETH price moves.

What is wei?

Wei is the smallest unit of Ethereum. 1 wei equals 0.000000000000000001 ETH (1e-18 ETH).

How many wei are in one ETH?

There are exactly 1,000,000,000,000,000,000 wei (1e18) in 1 ETH.

Is the ETH price updated in real time?

This tool uses the latest available market data and you can refresh the price to update the estimate.

Can I convert other currencies to ETH?

Yes — use USD, GBP, JPY, and CNY pages in the converter hub.
