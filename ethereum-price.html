What drives the Ethereum price?

  • Supply & demand: buyers vs sellers across global exchanges.
  • Network activity: demand for block space and smart contract usage.
  • Fees & burn: higher usage can increase fees; part of fees are burned.
  • Macro environment: risk appetite, interest rates, USD liquidity.
  • Upgrades & news: protocol changes, adoption, regulation narratives.

FAQ

What is the Ethereum (ETH) price?

The Ethereum price is the market value of 1 ETH in a currency like USD, EUR, or BTC.

Why does the ETH price move so much?

ETH can be volatile because crypto trades 24/7 and reacts fast to news, liquidity, and risk sentiment.

Is ETH price the same everywhere?

Usually yes across major exchanges, but small differences can happen due to fees and liquidity.
