Ethereum Price Today

This page shows the current Ethereum (ETH) price and explains how the price of Ethereum changes based on market activity and network usage.

What Is the Ethereum Price?

The Ethereum price represents the market value of one ETH. It fluctuates depending on supply, demand, network activity, and global market conditions.

Why Does Ethereum Price Change?

Ethereum price changes are influenced by transaction demand, gas fees, adoption of decentralized applications, and overall sentiment in the crypto market.
